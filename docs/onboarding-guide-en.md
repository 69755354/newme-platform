# NewMe Business Platform · Getting Started

> This is not a feature doc. It tells each person what this system is for, what you need to do, and what to watch out for.

---

## For Tanya (CEO)

This is your daily operations cockpit. It turns what your sales team does into numbers you can understand at a glance.

**What you'll see**

Dashboard at a glance:
- How many leads, how many in progress, how many going cold
- Which salesperson is handling how many clients, who's been sitting on a client too long
- How many quotes sent, which ones haven't been signed, total value
- How much you spent on ads and how many leads came in

**What you must do**

Log in once a day. Glance at the dashboard. You don't need to operate anything — just see it.

## For Ayana (Admin)

You're the operations hub. Sales uses the system to track clients — you make sure everyone is using it, logging activity, and not making a mess.

**What you can do**

**Every day before day-end**, collect feedback from both salespeople — what they used today, what got stuck, anything that didn't feel right — and send SAM a one-liner summary.

## For Sales (Faheem / Mohamed)

This is your daily workbench. It replaces Excel and trying to remember everything.

**What you can do**
- See who to contact today (right on the dashboard)
- Track every client from first contact to signed deal — all on one timeline
- Create quotes directly in the system and send to clients
- Projects, payments, everything in one place
- No more digging through chat history and spreadsheets

**What you must do**

1. **Log every client interaction**: Find the person in the system, add an activity note — even one sentence is enough
2. **Manually advance the stage when it changes**: Done with quote? Drag to "Quotation" column. Received deposit? Drag to "Won"
3. **Before day-end, send Ayana one line**: What you used today, where you got stuck, or just "all good"

**Notes**
- Login: https://app.newme.ae
- Faheem: faheem@newme.ae
- Mohamed: mohamed@newme.ae
- Password: `123456` (change on first login)
- If something gets stuck or a stage looks wrong, tell Ayana — she'll sort it out
- If you don't log it, it didn't happen. When the boss looks at the data and sees your clients piling up with no activity — that means you didn't log, not that you didn't work
